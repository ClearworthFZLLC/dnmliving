DNM Living Properties L.L.C (“DNM Living”, “we”, “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, store and protect information when you visit our website, contact us or work with us on a property search or transaction.

By using this website or sharing your information with us, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Who we are

DNM Living is a boutique real estate brokerage headquartered in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. Our primary focus is residential property in the UAE, and we also assist clients considering property in other international markets.

2. Information we collect

We may collect information that you provide to us when you:

  • Submit an enquiry or consultation request through our website;
  • Contact us by email, telephone, messaging services or in person;
  • Arrange a consultation or property viewing; or
  • Engage us to assist with a property search, purchase or other transaction.

Depending on how you interact with us, this may include your name, country of residence, email address, telephone number, the purpose of your enquiry, indicative budget, preferred property type, timeline, and any other details you choose to share.

If you proceed with a transaction, we may also need to collect identification and verification information, such as passport, visa or Emirates ID details, proof of address and information about the source of funds. This is required so that we can meet our anti-money-laundering and customer due-diligence obligations under UAE law.

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited and general usage information.

3. How we use your information

We may use your information to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and arrange consultations;
  • Understand your requirements and identify, shortlist and compare suitable properties;
  • Arrange viewings and communicate with developers, sellers and other parties on your behalf;
  • Coordinate the brokerage process and the documentation involved in a transaction;
  • Carry out identity verification, due diligence and anti-money-laundering checks;
  • Send you property information or updates where you have agreed to receive them;
  • Improve our services and the functionality of our website; and
  • Comply with applicable legal, regulatory and contractual requirements.

We will only use your personal information for legitimate business purposes and in accordance with applicable laws. We will not send you marketing communications without your consent, and you may opt out at any time.

4. Sharing of personal information

DNM Living does not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

We may share your information where reasonably necessary to provide our services, operate our business or comply with legal obligations. This may include sharing information with:

  • Developers, sellers and their agents in connection with properties you have expressed interest in;
  • Banks, mortgage providers, conveyancers, trustees and legal advisers, where you ask us to involve them;
  • Partner brokers or agents in other countries, where you are considering property outside the UAE;
  • Trusted technology, hosting, communication and other service providers who support our business; and
  • The Dubai Land Department, the Real Estate Regulatory Agency and other government authorities, regulators, courts or law-enforcement bodies, where required or permitted by law.

Where we work with third parties, we take reasonable steps to ensure that your information is handled appropriately and securely.

5. International transfers

Because we serve clients from many countries and may assist with property outside the UAE, your information may be transferred to, stored in or accessed from countries other than the one in which you live. Where this happens, we take reasonable steps to ensure your information remains protected in line with applicable law.

6. Cookies and website technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve functionality, understand how visitors use the website and enhance the user experience. These may collect information such as your browser type, pages visited, approximate location and interactions with the website.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect how parts of the website work.

7. Third-party websites

Our website may contain links to websites or services operated by third parties, including developers and property portals. DNM Living is not responsible for the privacy practices, security or content of those websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

8. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure. However, no website, electronic transmission or method of storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of information transmitted over the internet.

9. Retention of information

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, provide our services, maintain business and transaction records, resolve disputes, and meet legal and regulatory requirements, including record-keeping obligations that apply to real estate brokers in the UAE.

When personal information is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to securely delete or anonymise it.

10. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable UAE laws and regulations, including the UAE’s personal data protection law where it applies, you may have rights regarding your personal information. These may include the right to request access to, correction of or deletion of certain information we hold about you, and the right to withdraw consent to marketing at any time.

Some information may need to be kept to meet legal or regulatory obligations even if you ask us to delete it. To exercise your rights, please contact us using the details below.

11. Children’s privacy

Our website and services are not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 18 through this website.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, business practices, technology or applicable laws. Any updated version will be published on this page and will take effect from the date of publication. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
